Must-Know Vocabulary 1

Health Care and the Health Care System

Teacher’s exact definition
HEALTH CARE
Simple meaning: Any service provided to individuals or communities that promote, maintain, and restore health.
What it really means: Everyone needs health care at some point.
Easy example: A yearly checkup or a flu shot.
Remember: Health care = services that promote, maintain, or restore health.
Teacher’s exact definition
HEALTH CARE SYSTEM
Simple meaning: All medical care available to a nation’s people, the way they receive the care, and the way the care is paid for.
What it really means: It includes people, care delivery, and payment.
Easy example: Providers, hospitals, and insurance are parts of the system.
Remember: System = care + receiving care + paying for care.
⭐ Remember: The health care system has two goals: prevent and cure disease and injury.
Must-Know Vocabulary 2

Primary and Allied Providers

Teacher’s exact definition
PRIMARY CARE PROVIDER
Simple meaning: Health care professional who provides checkups and general care.
What it really means: Your own doctor may be your primary care provider.
Easy example: A pediatrician provides health care for people under 21.
Remember: Primary = CHECKUPS + GENERAL care.
Teacher’s exact definition
ALLIED HEALTH PROFESSIONAL
Simple meaning: Medical professionals who perform duties which would otherwise have to be performed by doctors or nurses.
What it really means: They work under supervision and provide care.
Easy example: Nurses, pharmacists, health educators, counselors, mental health specialists, dentists, and nutritionists.
Remember: Allied professionals HELP provide care.
⭐ Remember: Primary care gives general care; allied professionals perform important health duties.
Must-Know Vocabulary 3

Preventive Care and Specialists

Teacher’s exact definition
PREVENTIVE CARE
Simple meaning: Steps taken to keep disease or injury from happening or getting worse.
What it really means: It finds problems while they are minor and teaches ways to prevent illness and injury.
Easy example: Regular yearly checkups and immunizations.
Remember: Preventive = STOP or FIND EARLY.
Teacher’s exact definition
SPECIALIST
Simple meaning: Health care professional trained to treat a special category of patients or specific health.
What it really means: The lesson explains that specialists treat special groups or specific health problems.
Easy example: A cardiologist treats heart problems.
Remember: Specialist = SPECIAL patients or problems.
⭐ Remember: A primary care provider may send you to a specialist.

Must-Know Vocabulary 4

Hospice Care

Teacher’s exact definition
HOSPICE CARE
Simple meaning: Care provider to terminal ill that focuses on comfort, not cure.
What it really means: Hospice workers are trained in pain management and emotional support for the patient and family.
Easy example: Care may be given at home or in an inpatient hospice facility.
Remember: Hospice = COMFORT, not cure.

Terminally ill patients have serious illnesses and will not recover.

⭐ Remember: Hospice supports the patient and family.
Must-Know Vocabulary 5

Health Insurance

Teacher’s exact definition
HEALTH INSURANCE
Simple meaning: A plan in which a person pays a set fee insurance company in return for the company’s agreement to pay some or all medical expenses when needed.
What it really means: A monthly fee helps the plan pay part of health care and sometimes prescription medicine costs.
Easy example: A family gets an insurance plan through an employer.
Remember: Pay a fee → insurance helps pay medical costs.

A coverage pool includes people who are well and people who are sick. Fees from people who are well help pay costs for people who are sick.

⭐ Remember: Health insurance helps with expensive medical costs.
Must-Know Vocabulary 6

Managed Care and HMO

Teacher’s exact definition
MANAGED CARE
Simple meaning: A health insurance plan that saves money by encouraging patients and providers to select less costly forms of care.
What it really means: Members agree to see doctors who participate in the plan.
Easy example: Choosing care from the plan’s participating providers.
Remember: Managed care = lower-cost choices.
Teacher’s exact definition
HEALTH MAINTENANCE ORGANIZATION (HMO)
Simple meaning: A health insurance plan that contracts with selected physicians and specialists to provide medical services.
What it really means: Members pay a fixed monthly rate and must use selected providers in the plan.
Easy example: Seeing a specialist who is part of the HMO.
Remember: HMO = SELECTED providers.
⭐ Remember: An HMO uses selected physicians and specialists.
Must-Know Vocabulary 7

PPO and POS Plans

Teacher’s exact definition
PREFERRED PROVIDER ORGANIZATION (PPO)
Simple meaning: A health insurance plan that allows its members to select a physical who participates in the plan or visit a physician of their choice.
What it really means: The lesson says members may choose a participating physician or a physician outside the plan, but usually pay more outside.
Easy example: Choosing an out-of-plan physician and paying more.
Remember: PPO = MORE provider choice.
Teacher’s exact definition
POINT-OF-SERVICE (POS) PLANS
Simple meaning: A health insurance plan that combines the features of HMOs and PPOs.
What it really means: Participating doctors cost less; nonparticipating doctors cost more.
Easy example: Choosing between a lower-cost plan doctor and a higher-cost outside doctor.
Remember: POS = HMO + PPO features.
⭐ Remember: PPO offers choice; POS combines HMO and PPO.
Important Comparison

HMO vs PPO vs POS

PlanMain ruleMemory clue
HMOUse selected physicians and specialists in the plan.Selected network

⭐ Remember: HMO = selected; PPO = choice; POS = combination.
Must-Know Vocabulary 8

Affordable Care Act

Teacher’s exact definition
AFFORDABLE CARE ACT (ACA)
Simple meaning: Law that is enacted in 2010to ensure that all U.S citizens have access to affordable care.
What it really means: The lesson says it offers health insurance plans to people in low-income jobs or those who are self-employed and is intended to reduce costs for uninsured people.
Easy example: A lower-income person may receive a subsidy to pay some insurance cost.
Remember: ACA = 2010 + access to affordable care.

Other government plans named in the material: Medicaid is for people who are unemployed; Medicare is for people over age 65 or who are disabled.

⭐ Remember: ACA was enacted in 2010.
Must-Know Vocabulary 9

Public Health

Teacher’s exact definition
PUBLIC HEALTH
Simple meaning: System to monitor the welfare of the population.
What it really means: The U.S. system monitors and promotes population welfare through hospitals, clinics, disease tracking, and research funding.
Easy example: County hospitals or free clinics provide low-cost or free care.
Remember: Public health = welfare of the POPULATION.

Government and nongovernment agencies both play important roles.

⭐ Remember: Public health focuses on communities and populations.
Must-Know Vocabulary 10

NIH and FDA

Teacher’s exact definition
NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF HEALTH (NIH)
Simple meaning: An agency of the federal government that funds medical research to enhance health, lengthen life, and reduce illnesses and disability.
What it really means: The lesson calls it the National Institutes of Health and says it supports more than 38,000 research projects nationwide.
Easy example: Supporting more than 38,000 research projects nationwide.
Remember: NIH = FUNDS medical research.
Teacher’s exact definition
FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION (FDA)
Simple meaning: A department of a federal government that regulates foods, drugs, and other substances.
What it really means: It assures the safety of foods, cosmetics, medicines, and medical devices.
Easy example: Assuring the safety of foods, cosmetics, medicines, and medical devices.
Remember: FDA = REGULATES and checks safety.
⭐ Remember: NIH funds research; FDA regulates products.
Must-Know Vocabulary 11

CDC

Teacher’s exact definition
CENTER FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION (CDC)
Simple meaning: An agency of the federal government that protects the health, safety, and security of Americans.
What it really means: The lesson calls it the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and says it monitors health and disease outbreaks and gives health information to the public.
Easy example: Monitoring a disease outbreak.
Remember: CDC = PROTECTS + MONITORS + INFORMS.
⭐ Remember: CDC protects health, safety, and security and tracks outbreaks.

Voluntary Health Organizations

Public Donations and Volunteers

These groups are not linked to the government. They collect public donations and encourage volunteers to raise funds. The money supports health education and research.

Examples

American Cancer Society

Key comparison: Voluntary groups use public donations; government organizations are government agencies.

⭐ Remember: Voluntary organizations rely on donations and volunteers.
International Public Health

WHO and Famine

The World Health Organization (WHO) is part of the United Nations and operates in 200 countries and territories. It provides information and aid, offers vaccinations, and provides famine relief.

Teacher’s exact definition
FAMINE
Simple meaning: A widespread shortage of food.
What it really means: It can lead to starvation and death for many people in a country.
Easy example: WHO provides famine relief to struggling communities and nations.
Remember: Famine = WIDESPREAD food shortage.

WHO relies on donations from the public to continue its work.

⭐ Remember: WHO works internationally with information, aid, vaccinations, and famine relief.

Things I Must Know for the Test

Questions and Answers

Why have regular checkups?
They reveal minor problems, help prevent problems, and maintain wellness.
Where is serious or emergency care given?
At a hospital.
How can people pay for care?
Health insurance, private insurance, employer plans, or government-supported care named in the lesson.
How can checkups reduce cost?
They can reveal health problems while they are minor.
Who treats a specific problem?
A specialist.
What do NIH, FDA, and CDC do?
NIH funds research; FDA regulates products; CDC protects, monitors, and informs.
